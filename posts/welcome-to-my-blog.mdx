Welcome, and thank you for visiting. This blog is a space where I'll share reflections on theology, apologetics, philosophy, and the intersection of faith and culture.

## Why a Blog?

In a world of short-form content and rapid-fire takes, I've felt a growing need for a place to think out loud — carefully, at length, and with some depth. That's what this blog is intended to be.

My hope is that these posts will be useful to:

- **Believers** who want to go deeper in their understanding of the Christian faith
- **Skeptics** who are willing to engage seriously with the intellectual case for Christianity
- **Pastors and ministry leaders** who are wrestling with how to lead their congregations through a post-Christian moment
- **Students** who are navigating the questions raised in university classrooms

## What to Expect

I'll write about a range of topics — everything from New Testament scholarship and the historicity of the resurrection, to the problem of evil, the nature of God, and the relationship between faith and reason.

Some posts will be more academic in tone. Others will be more devotional and pastoral. But all of them will be rooted in my conviction that the Christian faith is not only personally transformative but also historically grounded and intellectually credible.

I'm glad you're here. Let's think well together.
